WWF wildfire risk · Amazon · three simulation methods

Amazon Fire Lab

One problem, three lenses. The agent-based model shows how a single fire spreads through the landscape. The system dynamics model shows where the forest is heading until 2100. The discrete event model shows whether fire brigades can keep up during the fire season.

Agent-based core: Wilensky, U. (1997). NetLogo Fire model, CCL, Northwestern University; re-implemented and extended (degraded forest, drought, wind, spread speed, ignition types, rain, protected territory, firefighter agents, firebreak). System dynamics and discrete event models: own stylized models; parameters are illustrative assumptions, not calibrated forecasts. Context figures: Flores et al. (2024), Nature; Lovejoy & Nobre (2018), Science Advances.

WWF policy levers

Start values 2025: 74 % intact, 6 % degraded, 20 % cleared (Flores et al. 2024). Tipping when the rainfall index falls below 0.68; after that, dieback turns forest into savanna.

Causal loop diagram

R1 moisture recycling and R2 fire–degradation push the system towards the tipping point. B1 is the WWF lever: forest loss creates public pressure that cuts deforestation.
